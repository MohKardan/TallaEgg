Twenty-one non-merge commits have landed on `main` since `dd7c0fa` (`2026-10-06`, the commit audited by `AUDIT_2026-10c.md`; `20` after `a58d6e1`, the commit that archived `AUDIT_2026-10b` and `10c`). They split cleanly into two halves with opposite risk trajectories:

All **1,243** xUnit tests pass on clean Debug and Release builds with **0 warnings**, and `Wallets.Balance` on every ledger-bearing wallet row in the local SQL Server database (**197 of 217** rows; 20 untouched zero-balance rows) reconciles to the 8th decimal place against its latest `Transactions.BallanceAfter`.
2. **A new Next.js 14 web portal (`web/`, PRs `#350`, `#351`, `#353`) shipped outside CI and introduced one Critical and two High defects, alongside one newly reproduced High defect in the bot's dealer-quote confirmation flow:**
   - **[C-10] Critical:** `web/package-lock.json` ships end-of-life `next@14.2.35` (`next <=16.3.0-preview.10`), carrying 26 published advisories (`npm audit` exit `1`), including **Critical** unauthenticated Windows Remote Code Execution (`GHSA-p293-qw3h-jr36`) and **Critical** Image Optimization RCE (`GHSA-2464-fhr4-w9xw`, `GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4`) with default `/_next/image` enabled and 5 SVGs in `web/public/images/`, plus High `postcss <=8.5.22` advisories.
   - **[H-10] High (reproduced):** Accepting a dealer quote in the Telegram bot (`POST /api/quotes/accept`) sends neither `QuoteId` nor the confirmed price, and the endpoint returns only `ApiResponse<Guid?>` (the trade ID, no executed price). When a plausible quote update (up to ±5% every 2 minutes) lands before the user taps Confirm, `Orders.Api` locks and charges the **new** price while `BotHandler` sends a `TradeExecutedMessage` formatted from `orderState.Price` claiming the **old** price (`900,000 IRT` unannounced slippage reproduced on a `1 g` fill).
   - **[H-11] High (reproduced):** `web/lib/api/wallet-client.ts` subtracts `LockedBalance` from `Balance` a second time (`Wallet.LockBalance` already moved locked funds out of `Balance`) and `web/app/wallet/page.tsx` labels `Balance` as total balance (`موجودی کل`). A customer with `15,000,000 IRT` total (`5,000,000` free, `10,000,000` locked) sees **`-5,000,000 IRT` available** and **`5,000,000 IRT` total**; `BTC` is truncated to 4 decimals (`0.00004 BTC` -> `۰`); and real database assets `CREDIT_SEKE_BAHAR` and `CREDIT_BTC` fall back to raw English identifiers.
   - **[H-12] High (reproduced):** `web/`'s auth rate limiter trusts spoofable `X-Forwarded-For` headers and shares a 5,000-key FIFO map with per-Telegram-ID lockouts, allowing 5,500 spoofed headers to evict an active `auth:id:<telegramId>` lockout immediately; stateless 4-hour `zarorbit_session` cookies never re-check `Users.Api`, so suspended or blocked users retain portal access for up to 4 hours; `POST /api/auth/telegram` lacks `Origin` CSRF validation; and numeric `UserRole` enums serialize as `"0"` instead of role names.
   - **[H-6] High (tracked as `#36`, reproduced):** Cross-service credit validation (`ValidateCreditAndBalanceAsync` -> `LockBalanceAsync`) remains non-atomic; concurrent `AcceptQuoteAsync` calls reproduced a 2x credit ceiling overspend (`-46,000,000 IRT` locked against a `23,000,000 IRT` ceiling). Single-instance Telegram polling was verified to run sequentially (`maxConcurrent = 1`), keeping `H-6` contained on the bot alone until `web/` adds order entry.

Because `C-10` caps Security at `3.0 / 10` and three unresolved High findings (`H-6`, `H-10`, `H-11`) cap Financial Integrity at `5.0 / 10`, the unweighted mean across the five categories is **6.0 / 10** (production readiness **~58%**).
